## Favorite Series: *Attack on Titan*
**Attack on Titan** (*Shingeki no Kyojin*) is an anime by **Hajime Isayama** about humanity's fight for survival against giant creatures called **Titans**.

## Why I love it:
- The story is intense and emotional.
- The animation and soundtrack are amazing.
- It shows courage, sacrifice, and freedom.

## Main Characters:
1. **Eren Yeager** - vows to destroy all Titans.
2. **Mikasa Ackerman** - loyal and powerful protector.
3. **Armin Arlert** - intelligent and strategic thinker.
4. **Levi Ackerman** - humanity's strongest soldier.

> "If you win, you live. If you lose, you die. If you don't fight, you can't win!" - *Eren Yeager*
---

## Summary:
*Attack on Titan* is not just about survival — it's about **freedom, sacrifice, and the truth behind humanity's existence**.
The story's twists, world-building, and emotional depth make in one of the **greatest anime series ever created**.
---

## Fun Fact:
The series first aired in **April 2013** and concluded in **November 2023**, leaving a lasting impact on anime fans around the world.
